Privacy Policy

Reel Engine · Effective 25 October 2026

Reel Engine is a private tool used by one independent musician (the "owner") to publish their own content to their own Instagram and YouTube accounts. It is not offered to the public. This page explains what data it uses and how.

Data Reel Engine accesses

  • YouTube (Google): the owner's channel name and ID, permission to upload videos to that channel, and basic statistics for those videos (such as views and subscriber count).
  • Instagram (Meta): the owner's account name and ID, permission to publish reels, stories and posts, the comments and messages on those posts, and their insights (such as views and reach).

How the data is used

  • To publish content the owner has approved, to the owner's own accounts.
  • To show the owner how their own posts perform.
  • To help the owner reply to comments on their own posts. Replies are sent only after the owner approves them.

The data is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to build profiles of anyone.

Where it is stored

Sign-in tokens and statistics are stored on the owner's private server, which is not publicly reachable. Disconnecting an account in Reel Engine deletes its token from the server.

Google and YouTube

Reel Engine uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Reel Engine's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can remove Reel Engine's access to a Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Instagram

You can remove Reel Engine's access to an Instagram account at any time in Instagram: Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites.
