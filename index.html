Reel Engine

A private publishing tool for an independent musician.

Reel Engine is used by the independent musician behind @skyyb.oii to prepare short videos, lyric videos and posts from their own songs, and to publish them to their own accounts on Instagram and YouTube.

What it does

  • Makes vertical reels, stories and full lyric videos from the artist's own music.
  • Publishes them to the artist's own accounts, only after the artist approves each one.
  • Reads view and follower numbers for those accounts so the artist can see what works.

Who can use it

Reel Engine is not a public service. Only its owner uses it, with their own accounts. It does not sign up other users and does not post on anyone else's behalf.
